Installing it

  1. Unzip the file

    sam-extension.zip, sent alongside this page. Put the folder somewhere it can stay — Chrome loads it from where it sits, so deleting the folder uninstalls the extension.

  2. Open your extensions page

    Type chrome://extensions into the address bar (edge://extensions on Edge) and press Enter.

  3. Turn on Developer mode

    Top-right corner of that page.

  4. Load unpacked

    A button appears top-left. Click it and pick the folder you unzipped — the one with manifest.json in it, not its parent.

  5. Switch it on for your dialer

    Open VICIdial, click the S.A.M icon in the toolbar, and turn it on for that site. It stays off everywhere you have not switched it on.

How the loan amount is worked out

Six lines, added, then grossed up. Every figure below is editable in Settings — they are this floor's numbers, not the law.

LineWhere it comes from
EscrowsMost recent year's property tax off the Zillow page, plus $1,000 for insurance, halved — six months
Title fees$1,500 flat
Mortgage payoffThe application
Cash to borrowerThe application
Appraisal$700 — waived on a VA IRRRL, which reuses the existing valuation
Underwriting$2,000 flat
Gross-upThe total × 1.035

If any line is missing, there is no loan amount at all — the box shows a dash and names what it is waiting on. A figure quietly missing its escrows goes into Easy Qualifier and prices a loan that cannot close, which is worse than an empty box.

Seven fields on the application decide the answer — full name, mortgage balance, FICO, cash-out, value, monthly income and loan type — and they sit on a darker ground so a blank among them is visible at a glance. The rest is worth having and none of it stops a quote.

The panel shows the loan amount, its working, and the debt ratio, and stops there. Everything else Easy Qualifier asks for still goes on the clipboard with Copy — it just no longer stands between you and the two figures you read out loud.

These are the same four charges that separate the advertised figure from the take-home figure at the top of the panel. One set of numbers, edited in one place, so the two halves cannot quote different fees on the same screen.

What it assumes without asking

Three standing assumptions, marked on the panel with an amber dot so you can see they were not read off anything. Correct them in Easy Qualifier on the files where they are wrong.

FieldAssumedWhy
OccupancyPrimary ResidenceThis floor refinances people in the house they live in
Property TypeSingle family residenceSame reason
VA Use TypeSubsequent useWorth 1.15 points of funding fee — 2.15% first use against 3.30% after. Assuming the cheaper one under-quotes every file it gets wrong, so the equity figures use the subsequent tier too.

Things it refuses to do quietly

Quote a loan that cannot be written

The loan is sized from what the borrower needs and never looks at the house. If that lands past the programme's LTV cap, it says so in red with the gap — the two halves of the panel are computed independently and you should not be the one who spots the disagreement.

Treat blank as zero

An empty cash-out box is an unanswered question, not a rate-and-term refinance. An empty tax figure means no loan amount, not free escrows.

Believe a yearly income

$88,800 typed where $7,400 belongs makes every ratio look twelve times better. Under 8% front-end at a real payment gets flagged.

Read a tax figure that is not there

The property tax comes out of Zillow's payload, in both the plain and the escaped spelling the live page actually uses. No figure means no escrow line and no loan amount — never a silent zero.

Estimate only. Not a quote, an offer, or a commitment to lend, and every figure on the panel is a working number for a phone call rather than a Loan Estimate.
