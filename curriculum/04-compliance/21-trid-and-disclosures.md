# TRID and Disclosures

**As of August 2026.** The paperwork rules. Boring until you break one, at which point it
becomes a cure, a refund, or a lawsuit.

---

## What TRID is

**TRID** = TILA-RESPA Integrated Disclosure. It merged four old forms into two:

| Form | When | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Loan Estimate (LE)** | Within **3 business days** of application | What the loan will cost |
| **Closing Disclosure (CD)** | At least **3 business days** before consummation | What it actually costs |

Both are standardized. The borrower can lay yours next to a competitor's and compare
line by line — which is exactly why "send me their LE" is the right move when someone
quotes you a better rate (`24-the-never-say-list.md`).

---

## The six things that trigger an application

This is the rule new LOs break constantly, usually without noticing.

Once you have collected **all six** of these, you have an **application** under TRID, and
the 3-business-day LE clock starts:

1. Borrower's **name**
2. Borrower's **income**
3. Borrower's **Social Security number** (to pull credit)
4. **Property address**
5. **Estimated property value**
6. **Loan amount sought**

**Look at what that means for your cold calls.** In Call 1, the originator collects the
name, the property address, the estimated value ("conservative 550"), the loan amount
("about $120"), and the income ("base pays around $72"). That is **five of six**. The
only missing element is the SSN.

He is one question away from triggering a federal disclosure obligation, on a call he
thinks of as a casual quote.

> **The practical rule:** know exactly where that line is. Either stay clearly on the
> pre-application side ("I'm not taking an application yet, just seeing if this is worth
> your time"), or cross it deliberately and get the LE out in three business days. What
> you must never do is cross it accidentally.

## Timing rules you have to know

| Rule | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **LE delivery** | Within 3 business days of application, and no later than 7 business days before consummation |
| **CD delivery** | Received by borrower at least 3 business days before consummation |
| **Mailbox rule** | If mailed/emailed, deemed received 3 business days after sending |
| **Rate lock** | Re-disclose LE within 3 business days of lock if terms change |
| **Right of rescission** | Refinance on a **primary residence** — borrower has **3 business days after closing** to cancel |

"Business day" has two different definitions in TRID depending on the rule (general vs.
"specific"). This trips up experienced people. When it matters, ask your processor —
don't guess.

## Tolerances — the part that costs your company money

Fee estimates on the LE are held to tolerance. Exceed them without a valid reason and
your company **cures** — refunds the difference to the borrower.

| Tolerance | Applies to | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Zero tolerance** | Your origination charges, points, lender fees, transfer taxes | Cannot increase at all |
| **10% cumulative** | Services borrower can shop for, from your provided list; recording fees | Total may rise up to 10% |
| **No tolerance** | Prepaids, escrow deposits, services shopped outside your list | May change |

**Why you care:** you generate zero-tolerance numbers when you quote. Sloppy estimating
means your branch eats cures, and that comes out of somebody's pocket — often yours.

### Changed circumstance

Tolerances can reset for a **valid changed circumstance**: new information, a borrower
request, a rate lock, an expired LE, or a genuine change in the transaction.

It must be **documented at the time**. "The rate went up" is not a changed circumstance.
"Borrower disclosed a second property, which changed the occupancy and pricing" is.

## Other disclosures in the file

| Disclosure | Trigger |
|---|---|
| **Intent to Proceed** | Borrower must affirmatively express it before you collect any fee beyond a credit report |
| **Home Loan Toolkit** | Purchase transactions, with the LE |
| **List of Service Providers** | If borrower may shop for services |
| **Appraisal delivery (ECOA)** | Copies "promptly upon completion," no later than **3 business days before consummation** |
| **Adverse Action Notice** | Within **30 days** of a denial — see `22-respa-ecoa-udaap-fair-lending.md` |
| **Safe Harbor / ATR-QM** | Ability to Repay documentation on every loan |

## Ability to Repay — the rule behind the questions

Since 2014, you must make a **reasonable, good-faith determination** the borrower can
repay. That's why the interrogation exists. The eight required ATR factors:

1. Current or expected income and assets
2. Current employment status
3. Monthly payment on this loan
4. Monthly payment on simultaneous loans
5. Monthly payment for mortgage-related obligations (taxes, insurance, HOA)
6. Current debt obligations, alimony, child support
7. Monthly debt-to-income ratio or residual income
8. Credit history

**A Qualified Mortgage (QM)** meets defined standards and carries legal protection for
the lender. The Price-Based General QM standard ties QM status to how far the loan's APR
sits above the Average Prime Offer Rate (APOR).

**Non-QM** loans (`02-products/10-non-qm.md`) are not illegal — they simply lack that
safe harbor, so lenders price for the added risk. Call 4's self-employed trucker with
unfiled returns is a non-QM borrower.

## How this shows up in your sales conversation

Disclosure rules are a **selling advantage** if you understand them.

> ✅ *"You'll get a Loan Estimate within three days — it's a federal form, same layout
> from every lender. Page two, section A is what I'm charging you. Compare mine to
> theirs line by line. I'm not worried about that comparison; the guys who bury a point
> and a half in discount are."*

Confidence in the paperwork reads as competence. It also gets you the competitor's LE,
which is what you needed.

## What you must be able to do

- List the six elements that trigger an application
- Recognize when a cold call is about to cross that line
- Explain the LE and CD timing rules
- Explain what a tolerance cure is and why sloppy quoting causes one
- Use the standardized LE as a competitive weapon

**Self-check:** On a first call you get the name, property address, income, estimated
value, and desired loan amount, then ask for the SSN to pull credit. What just happened?

> *You took an application. The 3-business-day LE clock started. Tell your processor
> today, not Friday.*
