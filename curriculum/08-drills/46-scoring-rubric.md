# The Call Scorecard

The 110-point rubric used in `../07-teardowns/` and by the `/call-score` skill. Score
your own calls against the same standard the transcripts were judged by.

---

## How to use it

1. **Record your calls** where legally permitted
   (`../04-compliance/19-cold-calling-law.md` on two-party consent)
2. Transcribe one per week — any transcription tool works
3. Score it honestly against this rubric
4. **Pick the single lowest category** and work only on that for a week
5. Re-score in two weeks

**One category at a time.** Trying to fix everything fixes nothing.

---

## The rubric

### 1. Opening (10)

| Points | Standard |
|---|---|
| 9–10 | Name confirm + pause, name/company/NMLS, **specific true reason**, easy question. Under 20 seconds. |
| 7–8 | Most elements present, minor stumbles |
| 5–6 | Identified self and reason, but generic or slightly evasive |
| 3–4 | Pitched in the opening, or weak permission ask |
| 0–2 | **False or misleading reason for the call** |

**Auto-cap at 3** if the lead source is misrepresented.

### 2. Frame (10)

| Points | Standard |
|---|---|
| 9–10 | Set the agenda, bounded the time, pre-framed a possible "no", got a small yes |
| 6–8 | Some framing |
| 3–5 | Minimal |
| 0–2 | None — went straight to questions |

### 3. Discovery — Facts (10)

Balance, value, rate, term, product, income, employment, credit, debts, occupancy.

| Points | Standard |
|---|---|
| 9–10 | Complete, efficient, well-sequenced |
| 6–8 | Most collected |
| 3–5 | Significant gaps |
| 0–2 | Almost none |

### 4. Discovery — Drivers (10)

**The category most calls fail.**

| Points | Standard |
|---|---|
| 9–10 | **Asked what the money is for**, what happens if they don't get it, timeline, decision-makers, and what a successful outcome looks like |
| 6–8 | Asked purpose, explored it somewhat |
| 3–5 | Asked purpose, dropped it immediately |
| 0–2 | **Never asked what the money is for** |

### 5. Diagnose (10)

Did you tell them one true thing they didn't know?

| Points | Standard |
|---|---|
| 9–10 | Corrected a wrong belief, explained something useful, changed how they see their situation |
| 6–8 | Explained something genuinely useful |
| 3–5 | Some education |
| 0–2 | Nothing taught, or **reinforced a false belief** |

**Auto-zero** if you implied you could approach a legacy rate.

### 6. Position (10)

| Points | Standard |
|---|---|
| 9–10 | Tied the solution to **their own stated words**, got a reaffirming yes |
| 6–8 | Positioned against a stated goal |
| 3–5 | Generic positioning |
| 0–2 | Pitched product with nothing to position against |

### 7. Product Knowledge (10)

| Points | Standard |
|---|---|
| 9–10 | Right product identified, alternatives priced, tradeoffs explained honestly |
| 6–8 | Right product, alternatives mentioned |
| 3–5 | Reasonable product, alternatives ignored |
| 0–2 | **Wrong product**, or the obviously correct one never mentioned |

**In this market, if the borrower holds a sub-4.5% rate and you didn't raise a second
lien, you cannot score above 3.**

### 8. Tonality (10)

Inferred from transcript tells — filler, interruptions, monologue length, hedging.

| Points | Standard |
|---|---|
| 9–10 | Varied, controlled, appropriate pacing, comfortable with silence |
| 6–8 | Generally good, some filler |
| 3–5 | Heavy filler, rushed, or flat |
| 0–2 | Talked over the borrower repeatedly, could not tolerate silence |

### 9. Listening (10)

| Points | Standard |
|---|---|
| 9–10 | Let them finish, reflected content back, followed the emotion, went silent on disclosures |
| 6–8 | Generally listened |
| 3–5 | Acknowledged with filler, missed threads |
| 0–2 | **Interrupted a material disclosure**, or ignored what they told you |

**Auto-cap at 2** if the borrower stated their time horizon or decision criterion and you
kept selling something else.

### 10. Close / Next Step (10)

| Points | Standard |
|---|---|
| 9–10 | Asked for a specific action, and locked **time + task + channel** |
| 6–8 | Asked, booked a next contact, missing one element |
| 3–5 | Vague next step |
| 0–2 | **No close attempted**, or the borrower took control of follow-up |

### 11. Compliance (10)

| Points | Standard |
|---|---|
| 10 | NMLS given, recording disclosed, truthful throughout, no rate promised, revocation honored |
| 7–9 | Minor omissions (e.g. no NMLS) |
| 4–6 | Misleading framing, or a qualification claim without caveats |
| 1–3 | **Rate promised before pricing**, or competitor disparagement |
| **0** | **False lead-source claim, or continued past a revocation** |

**Compliance is not averaged with the rest.** See below.

## Scoring

**Total: /110**

| Score | Grade | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 95–110 | A | Elite. Advisor-level. |
| 80–94 | B | Strong. Closing consistently. |
| 65–79 | C | Competent. Leaking deals on specifics. |
| 50–64 | D | Sales instincts present, knowledge missing |
| Under 50 | F | Structural problems |

### The compliance override

**If Compliance scores 0, the call fails regardless of total.**

You can run a beautiful call and still have committed a TCPA violation. Paula's call
(Teardown 2) scored 8/10 on tonality and 0 on compliance. It is a failed call.

**Write "FAIL — COMPLIANCE" at the top and fix that first.** Nothing else matters until
it's fixed.

## The quick version

For scoring in ninety seconds:

```
□ Did I tell the truth about why I was calling?           (if no → FAIL)
□ Did I honor any revocation?                             (if no → FAIL)
□ Did I promise a rate before pricing?                    (if yes → FAIL)
□ Did I ask what the money is for?
□ Did I handle their existing rate honestly?
□ Did I raise the second lien (if legacy rate)?
□ Did I shut up when they disclosed something?
□ Did I tell them one true thing they didn't know?
□ Did I close?
□ Did I lock time + task + channel?
```

**Ten boxes. Three of them are pass/fail.** If you're checking eight or more consistently,
you're in the top decile of mortgage cold callers.

## The weekly protocol

**Monday:** score last week's recorded call. Identify the lowest category.

**Tuesday–Friday:** work only on that category. One variable.

**Following Monday:** re-score. Did it move?

**Monthly:** review four weeks of scores. Which category never improves? That's the one
that needs a different approach — go to the linked module and re-read it.

Track it:

