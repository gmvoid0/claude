# What This System Is Lacking

An honest inventory of what the coaching does **not** know, ranked by how much each gap is
currently costing the quality of advice you get.

Fill these in and the coaching improves immediately. Several of them cannot be worked
around — they can only be supplied.

---

## Tier 1 — These materially degrade the coaching right now

### 1. Audio of your own calls

**The single biggest gap.**

The entire source material asserts that **tonality outranks content** — "how you say
something is even more important than what you say." Then it delivers that lesson as text.

I have four transcripts. I have zero tonality data. No pitch, no pace, no warmth, no
hesitation, no silence. I inferred tonality problems in the transcripts from *textual
tells* — filler frequency, interruptions, monologue length, hedging — which is a proxy,
not a measurement.

**What this means practically:** I can teach tonality theory and prescribe drills. I
**cannot** diagnose yours. If you have uptalk, if you rush numbers, if you sound young, if
you can't tolerate silence — I won't know unless you tell me.

**What to supply:** recordings of your own calls, or transcripts of them plus your honest
self-assessment. Where recording is legally permitted
(`../04-compliance/19-cold-calling-law.md`), this is the highest-value thing you can give
this system.

> **Note the asymmetry:** everything in this repo is about *their* calls. The coaching gets
> dramatically better when it's about yours.

---

### 2. Which states you're licensed in — and which states your lists cover

**These are two different questions and the second one governs.**

You're multi-state licensed. Mini-TCPA exposure is state-specific and severe:

- **Florida, Oklahoma, Maryland, Washington, New Jersey** all have active statutes
- Calling windows differ (FL and MD stop at **8 p.m.**, an hour before federal)
- Daily contact caps differ (FL, OK, MD cap at **3 attempts**)
- Damages run **$500–$20,000 per violation**, with private rights of action

Until I know your states, `../04-compliance/19-cold-calling-law.md` ships generic, and I've
told you to build to the strictest standard as a workaround. That's safe but it costs you
dials.

**What to supply:** your licensed states, and separately, the states your lead lists
actually cover. Then I can finish the compliance matrix — license number, renewal date, CE
requirement, calling hours, mini-TCPA status, advertising rules — as a single page.

---

### 3. Your lender panel

"Over 50 lenders" is a sales line, not information. Without names I can teach that lender
selection is the core broker skill (`../01-industry/03-channels-and-your-seat.md`) but I
cannot teach you to *do* it.

**What to supply:** lender names, your AE contacts, each lender's niche, current turn
times, and known overlays.

**What that unlocks:** "for this borrower, submit to X because Y" instead of "ask your
AE." Specifically it would let me answer Call 3 and Call 4's scenarios concretely rather
than in principle.

---

### 4. Your compensation plan

I've used **150 bps on a $250,000 average loan** as an illustration throughout. It's
almost certainly wrong for you.

**What to supply:** your bps, whether it's tiered by volume, LPC vs. BPC and who decides,
per-file or desk fees, W-2 vs. 1099, and when you're paid after funding.

**What that unlocks:** the KPI math in `../09-business/48-kpi-math.md` becomes *your*
plan rather than an example. The daily dial number changes materially.

---

### 5. Your baseline metrics

I've used planning estimates for every conversion rate. Yours will differ substantially.

**What to supply:** two weeks of tracked dials, contacts, conversations, applications, and
talk time.

**What that unlocks:** real diagnosis. Right now I can tell you *how* to find your weakest
funnel stage. With your numbers I can tell you *which one it is* and what it's costing.

## Tier 2 — These limit specific kinds of advice

### 6. Your lead vendor's consent language

**This is where your actual TCPA liability lives**, and most LOs have never read it.

**What to supply:** the exact opt-in text consumers agreed to, whether consent is
documented per record with timestamp and IP, and whether the lists are DNC-scrubbed and
how recently.

Without this, `../06-cold-calling/35-aged-lead-playbook.md` can tell you the three-month
EBR rule but not whether your specific list clears it.

### 7. Your company's compliance manual

Company policy routinely exceeds legal minimums — recorded-line disclosure requirements,
NMLS recital rules, approved script language, prohibited claims.

I've written to the legal standard. **Your company's standard may be stricter, and theirs
governs.**

### 8. Your pricing engine and live rate sheets

I can teach how pricing works — LLPAs, rebate vs. discount, par
(`../01-industry/02-follow-the-money.md`). I cannot teach you to price without seeing real
grids.

**What that unlocks:** actual second-lien vs. cash-out comparisons with real numbers
instead of the illustrative ones in `../02-products/11-seconds-heloc-heloan.md`.

### 9. What you're actually approved to originate

Can you do non-QM? Bank statement? DSCR? VA? Second liens and HELOCs?

**This matters enormously** — Call 4 is a bank-statement deal and Call 1 is a second-lien
deal. If those aren't on your shelf, large parts of the playbook change.

### 10. Your CRM, LOS, and dialer

Arive? Encompass? Salesforce? Determines the actual daily workflow and whether the
tracking apparatus in `../08-drills/44-daily-protocol.md` can be automated.

### 11. Your current lead cost

Post-ban economics decide how hard you can work a lead before it stops paying, and whether
`../09-business/48-kpi-math.md`'s cost-per-funded-loan target is realistic for you.

### 12. Your manager's expectations

Quota, ramp period, floor time, minimums, and whether you're permitted to build outside
referral sources. Some shops restrict this.

## Tier 3 — Structural limits of this system

Things no amount of information fixes.

### 13. This is training, not legal advice

Every compliance claim here is stamped **as of August 2026** and sourced where possible.
Telemarketing and mortgage law move fast, and state law varies enormously.

**Confirm anything compliance-related with your compliance officer before you act on it.**
Items I could not verify are marked `[UNVERIFIED]`.

### 14. Facts rot

Rates, loan limits, guidelines, and funding fees all change. I've stamped dates on
everything so staleness is visible, but a document that was accurate in August 2026 may
mislead you in 2027.

**Re-verify any specific number before you put it in front of a borrower.**

### 15. I can't hear you, watch you, or sit next to you

The irreplaceable parts of sales training are a manager listening live, a mentor jumping
on a call, and the floor energy of a room full of people dialing. This system is a
supplement to those, not a replacement.

### 16. One statistic in the source material is unverified

The "**5 million more buyers per 1% rate drop**" figure in the boomerang technique comes
from the source video and is widely repeated. **I could not verify it.**

`../05-sales/29-objection-mastery.md` flags this. Use the qualitative version — falling
rates bring buyers back and increase competition, which is uncontroversially true — unless
you can source the number. Never cite a statistic you can't back up.
