# Glossary

Every term used in this system, plus the ones you'll hear on the floor. If a term appears
in this repo without definition, it should be here — and if it isn't, tell your coach.

---

## A

**Adverse Action Notice** — Required notice within 30 days of a denial or unaccepted
counteroffer, stating the specific principal reasons. Required by ECOA.

**AE (Account Executive)** — Your rep at a wholesale lender. Your most underused
relationship.

**AfBA (Affiliated Business Arrangement)** — Disclosure required when your company has an
ownership interest in a settlement service provider you refer to.

**AMC (Appraisal Management Company)** — Intermediary that orders appraisals, maintaining
appraiser independence.

**Amortization** — The schedule by which principal and interest are paid down to zero over
the loan term.

**APOR (Average Prime Offer Rate)** — Benchmark used to determine QM status and
higher-priced loan thresholds.

**Appraisal** — Independent opinion of property value. You may not influence it.

**Appraisal Waiver / PIW** — AUS-granted relief from requiring an appraisal.

**APR (Annual Percentage Rate)** — The note rate plus certain fees expressed as a rate.
Always higher than the note rate. A comparison tool, not the actual rate.

**ARM (Adjustable Rate Mortgage)** — Rate fixed for an initial period, then adjusting.
Written as 5/6, 7/6 — years fixed / months between adjustments.

**ATR (Ability to Repay)** — The rule requiring a reasonable good-faith determination the
borrower can repay. Eight required factors.

**AUS (Automated Underwriting System)** — DU (Fannie) or LPA (Freddie). Returns an
approval decision and a conditions list.

---

## B

**Basis point (bps)** — 0.01%. 100 bps = 1%. How compensation and pricing are quoted.

**BPC (Borrower-Paid Compensation)** — Broker paid directly by the borrower. Allows a
lower rate.

**Buydown** — Prepaid reduction of the rate. **Temporary** (2-1, 3-2-1) subsidizes the
payment for a period; **permanent** (points) lowers the note rate.

---

## C

**Cash-out refinance** — New larger loan; borrower receives the difference. Conventional
caps at 80% LTV on a primary residence.

**CD (Closing Disclosure)** — Final cost form. Must be received at least **3 business
days** before consummation.

**Changed circumstance** — A documented event permitting tolerance resets under TRID.

**Charge-off** — A debt the creditor has written off. Still owed.

**Clear to Close (CTC)** — All conditions satisfied; documents can be drawn.

**CLTV (Combined Loan to Value)** — All liens ÷ value. The governing number for second
liens.

**COE (Certificate of Eligibility)** — VA document establishing entitlement.

**Compensating factor** — A strength offsetting a weakness elsewhere in the file. Reserves
are the most powerful.

**Conditions** — Documentation required before approval, docs, or funding. PTA / PTD / PTF.

**Conforming** — Meets GSE standards including loan limits. 2026 baseline: **$832,750**.

**Conventional** — Not government-insured. Underwritten to Fannie or Freddie guidelines.

**Correspondent** — Lender that funds with its own line and sells to investors.

**Cure** — Refund to the borrower when a fee exceeds its TRID tolerance.

---

## D

**Deed of trust** — Security instrument used instead of a mortgage in some states.

**Depreciation add-back** — Non-cash deduction added back to self-employed qualifying
income. Often large for equipment-heavy businesses.

**Discount points** — Prepaid interest that permanently lowers the rate. 1 point = 1% of
the loan.

**DNC (Do Not Call)** — The National Registry. Must be scrubbed at least every 31 days.

**DSCR (Debt Service Coverage Ratio)** — Rental income ÷ PITIA. The qualifying metric for
investor loans that ignore personal income.

**DTI (Debt to Income)** — Monthly debts ÷ gross monthly income. Back-end is the one that
matters.

**DU (Desktop Underwriter)** — Fannie Mae's AUS.

---

## E

**EBR (Established Business Relationship)** — DNC exemption. **3 months** from an inquiry,
**18 months** from a transaction. The three-month rule is critical for aged leads.

**ECOA (Equal Credit Opportunity Act)** — Prohibits discrimination and discouragement.
Regulation B.

**Entitlement** — The amount VA guarantees. Determines zero-down capacity.

**Escrow / impound** — Account held by the servicer to pay taxes and insurance.

**Extenuating circumstances** — Documented non-recurring events that can shorten credit
event waiting periods.

---

## F

**Fannie Mae (FNMA)** — GSE that buys and securitizes conventional loans.

**FCRA (Fair Credit Reporting Act)** — Governs credit reporting. Amended by the HPPA to
ban trigger leads.

**FHA** — Federal Housing Administration. Insures loans; enables lower credit and down
payment.

**FICO** — Credit score, 300–850. Mortgage models typically score lower than consumer apps.

**Forbearance** — Temporary payment suspension. Materially affects future eligibility.

**Freddie Mac (FHLMC)** — GSE. Counterpart to Fannie.

**Funding fee** — VA's one-time fee in lieu of monthly MI. **Exempt for borrowers receiving
VA disability.**

---

## G

**Ginnie Mae (GNMA)** — Government agency guaranteeing securities backed by FHA, VA, and
USDA loans.

**Gift funds** — Down payment funds from an eligible donor. Requires a gift letter, donor
ability, and a transfer trail.

**Gross up** — Increasing non-taxable income for qualifying, commonly by 25%.

**GSE (Government-Sponsored Enterprise)** — Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac.

---

## H

**HELOAN** — Fixed-rate second mortgage. Lump sum, fixed payment.

**HELOC** — Revolving line secured by the home. Variable rate. Draw period then repayment
period.

**HMDA** — Home Mortgage Disclosure Act. Application-level reporting analyzed for
disparities.

**HPPA (Homebuyers Privacy Protection Act)** — Signed 9/5/2025, effective **3/5/2026**.
Banned credit-bureau trigger leads.

---

## I–L

**Intent to Proceed** — Borrower's affirmative statement required before collecting fees
beyond a credit report.

**IRRRL** — VA streamline refinance. No cash out. Minimal documentation.

**Jumbo** — Above the conforming limit. Held or privately securitized. Reserves-heavy.

**LE (Loan Estimate)** — Standardized cost form due within 3 business days of application.
**Always ask a competitor's borrower for theirs.**

**Lien** — Legal claim on property. First lien is paid first in foreclosure.

**LLPA (Loan-Level Price Adjustment)** — GSE risk-based pricing add-on. Driven by FICO,
LTV, occupancy, purpose, product.

**LO Comp Rule** — Reg Z rule barring compensation that varies with loan terms, dual
compensation, and steering.

**LOA (Loan Officer Assistant)** — Admin support. **Unlicensed LOAs may not take
applications, quote, or negotiate.**

**LOE (Letter of Explanation)** — Borrower's written explanation of anything unusual.

**LOS (Loan Origination System)** — The software the file lives in. Encompass, Arive,
others.

**LPA (Loan Product Advisor)** — Freddie Mac's AUS. **Run it when DU refers.**

**LPC (Lender-Paid Compensation)** — Broker paid from rate rebate. Borrower pays no
origination.

**LPMI (Lender-Paid MI)** — MI built into a higher rate. **Not cancellable.**

**LTV (Loan to Value)** — Loan ÷ value.

---

## M–N

**MAP Rule / Reg N** — Prohibits deceptive mortgage advertising.

**MBS (Mortgage-Backed Security)** — Bond backed by a pool of mortgages. **Where your rate
actually comes from.**

**MI (Mortgage Insurance)** — Protects the lender at higher LTV. PMI (conventional,
cancellable) or MIP (FHA, usually permanent).

**Mini-TCPA** — State telemarketing statutes stricter than federal. FL, OK, MD, WA, NJ.
$500–$20,000 per violation.

**MIP (Mortgage Insurance Premium)** — FHA's mortgage insurance. Upfront plus annual.
**Usually lasts the life of the loan.**

**MSA (Marketing Services Agreement)** — Payment for actual marketing services. Legal in
principle, heavily scrutinized. Never without compliance review.

**MSR (Mortgage Servicing Rights)** — The right to service a loan, bought and sold
separately.

**NMLS** — Nationwide Multistate Licensing System. Your license lives here, publicly.

**Non-QM** — Loans outside Qualified Mortgage parameters. Not subprime — alternative
documentation.

**Note** — The promise to repay. The mortgage is the lien securing it.

---

## O–P

**Overlay** — A lender's requirement stricter than the agency guideline. **Why a denial at
one lender means little.**

**Par** — Price of 100. No points, no rebate.

**PITI** — Principal, Interest, Taxes, Insurance. The real payment. **Always quote this.**

**PITIA** — PITI plus association dues.

**PMI (Private Mortgage Insurance)** — Conventional MI. **Cancellable at 80% LTV,
automatic at 78%.**

**Points** — 1% of the loan amount. Discount points lower the rate; negative points
(lender credits) raise it and cover costs.

**Pull-through** — Fundings ÷ applications.

---

## Q–R

**QM (Qualified Mortgage)** — Meets ATR safe harbor standards.

**Rapid rescore** — Expedited bureau update after a paydown. Used to cross a FICO tier
boundary.

**Rate lock** — Freezing the rate for a set period.

**Rebate** — Price above par. Funds available for comp or closing costs.

**Rescission** — 3-business-day right to cancel a refinance on a primary residence, after
closing.

**Reserves** — Liquid assets remaining after closing, in months of PITI. **The most
powerful compensating factor.**

**RESPA** — Real Estate Settlement Procedures Act. **Section 8** bars anything of value for
referrals. Criminal penalties.

**Revocation** — A consumer's withdrawal of consent to be contacted, by any reasonable
means. **Must be honored.**

**ROV (Reconsideration of Value)** — Request to revisit an appraisal with better comps.

---

## S–T

**SAFE Act** — Requires MLOs to be licensed or registered through NMLS.

**Seasoning** — Time an asset has been in an account (60 days) or since a credit event.

**Second lien** — A loan behind the first mortgage. **The defining product of the 2026
market.**

**Servicer** — Collects payments after closing. Often not the original lender.

**SOFR** — Secured Overnight Financing Rate. The index most current ARMs use.

**Sourcing** — Documenting where funds came from.

**Steering** — Directing a borrower to a loan that pays you more. Prohibited.

**TCPA** — Telephone Consumer Protection Act. Governs consent, autodialers, revocation.
$500/call, trebled to $1,500 for willful violations.

**Tolerance** — TRID limits on how much a quoted fee may increase. Zero / 10% / no
tolerance.

**Tradeline** — An account on a credit report.

**Tri-merge** — Credit report pulling all three bureaus. Middle score governs.

**Trigger lead** — Lead generated from a mortgage credit inquiry. **Banned as of 3/5/2026.**

**TRID** — TILA-RESPA Integrated Disclosure. Governs the LE and CD.

**TSR (Telemarketing Sales Rule)** — FTC rule governing DNC, calling hours, and required
disclosures.

---

## U–Z

**UDAAP** — Unfair, Deceptive, or Abusive Acts or Practices. **Deception requires neither
intent nor actual harm.**

**Underwriter** — Decides. Reviews the file against guidelines.

**UFMIP** — FHA's upfront mortgage insurance premium. Usually financed.

**USDA** — Zero-down loan for eligible rural areas and incomes. **Check the map before
ruling it out.**

**VA** — Department of Veterans Affairs loan. Zero down, no monthly MI, assumable.

**VOE (Verification of Employment)** — Confirmation of employment. Verbal VOE typically
required before closing.

**Warrantable** — A condo project meeting agency standards. Non-warrantable requires
specialty lending.

---

## The floor slang

**"Blew up"** — The deal died.
**"Buying the rate down"** — Paying points.
**"Clean file"** — No conditions expected.
**"Fallout"** — Locked loans that don't fund.
**"In the money"** — Enough rate benefit to justify a refi.
**"On the board"** — A funded loan.
**"Papered"** — Documented properly.
**"Pulled through"** — Made it to funding.
**"Rate sheet is ugly"** — Pricing worsened.
**"Refi boom"** — What everyone is waiting for.
**"Sitting on a three"** — Has a legacy rate. **The defining condition of this market.**
**"Turn times"** — How long the lender takes.
