# 1 — The math

```
P&I  = (Loan ÷ 1,000) × factor

  RATE    /$1,000 (30yr)        RATE    /$1,000 (30yr)
  3.0%      4.22                6.5%      6.32
  4.0%      4.77                6.75%     6.49   ←
  5.0%      5.37                7.0%      6.65   ←
  6.0%      6.00                7.5%      6.99

PITI          = P&I + taxes + insurance + MI + HOA
                taxes ≈ value × 1.0–1.5% ÷ 12

DTI           = total monthly debt ÷ gross monthly income
Max PITI      = (income × 0.45) − existing debts

LTV           = loan ÷ value
CLTV          = all liens ÷ value
Max cash-out  = (value × 0.80) − balance − costs

Blended rate  = total annual interest ÷ total balance
Break-even    = cost ÷ monthly savings
Gross-up      = non-taxable × 1.25
Bank stmt     = (deposits ÷ months) × 0.50
Rental        = gross rent × 0.75
```

# 2 — The compliance stop signs

```
STOP IMMEDIATELY:
  "Take me off your list"     "Stop calling"
  "Remove me"                 "Do not call"
  "I'm not interested" (2nd clear time)
  → "Understood, I'll take you off. Sorry to bother you."
  → LOG IT. Internal DNC. Permanently.

NEVER SAY:
  ✗ "You submitted an inquiry"  (unless they did)
  ✗ "I know I can beat that rate"  (before pricing)
  ✗ "My manager will approve it if we lock today"  (if untrue)
  ✗ "We take scores as low as 500"  (without the caveats)
  ✗ "They'll refuse to transfer the appraisal"
  ✗ Anything implying you can match a sub-4% legacy rate

ALWAYS SAY:
  ✓ Name, company, NMLS, recorded line
  ✓ The true reason for the call
  ✓ "I'll tell you if I can't beat it"

HOURS: 8am–8pm in THEIR time zone (strictest standard)
CAP:   3 attempts per person per day
DNC:   scrub every 31 days
EBR:   inquiry = 3 MONTHS · transaction = 18 months
```

# 3 — The call arc

```
1. OPEN     (15s)  Name? → who you are + NMLS → true reason → easy question
2. FRAME    (30s)  "Six questions, then I'll tell you yes or no. Fair?"
3. DISCOVER (50%)  What's the money for? What happens if you don't get it?
4. DIAGNOSE        Tell them one true thing they didn't know
5. POSITION        Tie back to THEIR words. Get the reaffirming yes.
6. PRESENT         Payment → benefit → delta → rate → SILENCE
7. CLOSE           Ask for the next small step. Then shut up.
8. LOCK            TIME + TASK + CHANNEL
```

# 4 — The legacy-rate script

**The most important script in this market.**

```
"What's the rate on your current mortgage?"
  → [3.1]

"Okay, stop me right there. Three-one is phenomenal. I can't beat it,
 nobody can — the market's at about six-eight. So this isn't a
 'lower your rate' call.

 The real question is whether we can get you what you need WITHOUT
 touching that rate. Two ways:

   1. Cash-out refi — you get the money, but you give up the 3.1%
      on your ENTIRE balance
   2. Second lien — first mortgage untouched, market rate only on
      the new money

 Usually option two is dramatically better. Can I price both?

 And tell me — what's the money actually for?"
```

# 5 — Objection quick reference

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| **"What's your rate?"** | "I'll get you a real number, not a fake one. Rate depends on score, equity, purpose, occupancy. Four questions?" |
| **"Already working with someone"** | "Good, you should be. Have you locked yet?" |
| **"Their rate is lower"** | "Could be. Send me their Loan Estimate — the actual form. If it beats mine I'll tell you to sign it." |
| **"Waiting for rates to drop"** | "Then a second is even better — get the money now, consolidate when they drop." |
| **"Need to think about it"** | "What else can I give you to help your thinking?" **[SILENCE]** |
| **"Need to ask my spouse"** | "What'll their first question be? Want me to get you both on for ten minutes?" |
| **"How'd you get my number?"** | "You filled out a request on [site] on [date]. Don't remember it? I'll take you off right now." |
| **"My credit's bad"** | "What's 'bad' mean to you? People say that about a 640 and a 540." |
| **"Send me something"** | "Sending now. I'll call at 3:00 to walk through it — those forms are dense." |
| **"Closing costs too high"** | "Three ways: pay them, roll them in, or I take a higher rate and the lender credits them. How long are you staying?" |
| **"Never heard of you"** | "Look me up while we talk. NMLS Consumer Access, my ID is [ ]." |
| **"Not interested"** | One reframe. **Second time = stop.** |

# 6 — Product fit

```
Sub-4.5% first + needs cash    → SECOND LIEN (almost always)
Moving in <3 years             → ARM + lender credits, no points
Self-employed, aggressive w/o  → BANK STATEMENT
Returns not filed              → BANK STATEMENT
Investor, DTI blown            → DSCR
Veteran                        → VA (check disability → funding fee exemption)
Retired, assets no income      → ASSET DEPLETION
FICO <620                      → FHA
FICO 620–680                   → RUN BOTH FHA & CONVENTIONAL
FICO 680+                      → CONVENTIONAL (cancellable PMI)
Zero down, rural-ish, mod inc  → CHECK USDA MAP FIRST
Cards at 20%+                  → DEBT CONSOLIDATION (run blended rate)
Seller won't drop price        → SELLER-PAID BUYDOWN
```

# 7 — Discovery questions

```
SITUATION
  How long in the house?
  Primary residence?
  Anyone else part of this decision?      ← prevents the spouse objection

PURPOSE  ← THE ONE EVERYONE SKIPS
  What got you looking at this?
  What would a successful outcome look like to you?
  WHAT'S THE MONEY FOR?
  Is there a timeline?

PAIN
  What happens if this doesn't get done?
  How long has that been a problem?
  What's it costing you monthly right now?

FACTS
  Balance · value · rate · term · type
  Income · employment type · how long
  Credit estimate · any recent events
  Other debts (balances AND rates)
  Taxes · insurance

DECISION
  Talking to anyone else?
  What would make you say yes today?
  What's most likely to stop this?
```

# 8 — Income interview

```
1. W-2, 1099, or self-employed?

   IF W-2:   Salary or hourly? OT/bonus/commission? How long there?
   IF SELF:  How long owned? Entity — sole prop, LLC, S-corp?
             Returns filed and current?
             → "In a normal month, how much is DEPOSITED into the
                business account? Gross deposits, not profit."

2. Any other income? Rental, retirement, disability, child support?
3. Any of it non-taxable?          ← gross up ×1.25
4. Any employment gaps in 2 years?

SELF-EMPLOYED ADD-BACKS:
  Depreciation ← the big one
  Depletion · Amortization · Business use of home · One-time losses
```

# 11 — The ten rules from the teardowns

```
 1. Say the rate truth first. "I can't beat your 3%. Nobody can."
 2. Always ask what the money is for.
 3. Know the second lien cold.
 4. Shut up when they disclose something.
 5. Match the product to their time horizon.
 6. A documentable credit event is a different animal — ask for paperwork.
 7. Never promise a rate before pricing.
 8. Protect their money before your commission.
 9. End with time + task + channel.
10. When compliance and the close conflict, compliance wins.
```
