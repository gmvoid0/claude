A

Stop signs

Read first

Compliance beats the close, every time. A lost deal costs you a Tuesday. A revoked license costs you the career.

Stop the call immediately
  • “Take me off your list”
  • “Stop calling me” / “Remove my number”
  • “This is harassment”
  • “I’m not interested” — second clear time

“Understood — I’ll take you off our list right
now. Sorry to have bothered you.”

→ Log it. Internal DNC. Permanently.

Never say

✗“You submitted an inquiry” — unless they actually did
✗“I know I can beat that rate” — before credit and pricing
✗“My manager will approve it if we lock today” — if untrue
✗“We take scores as low as 500” — without the 10%-down reality
✗“They’ll refuse to transfer the appraisal”
✗Anything implying you can approach a sub-4% legacy rate

Name · company · NMLS · recorded line — up front, and true.

B

The legacy-rate script

Most-used

Every borrower who transacted 2020–22 holds 2.5–4%. You cannot lower it. Saying so in the first minute is the highest-trust move available to you.

Ask first

“What’s the rate on your current mortgage?”

Then, immediately

“Okay, stop me right there. Three-one is phenomenal. I can’t beat it, nobody can — the market’s at about six-eight. So this isn’t a ‘lower your rate’ call. The real question is whether we can get you what you need without touching that rate. Two ways: 1 · Cash-out refi — you get the money, but you give up the 3.1% on your entire balance. 2 · Second lien — first mortgage untouched, market rate only on the new money. Usually option two is dramatically better. Can I price both? And tell me — what’s the money actually for?”

Why it works

It disqualifies you from the “another guy promising a lower rate” category in about eight seconds, and it plants the second-lien solution before they raise the objection. Across four real transcripts with ~$1.5M in combined equity behind legacy-rate first mortgages, the second lien was never mentioned once.

C

Objections

Filterable
What’s your rate?

“I’ll get you a real number, and I don’t want to give you a fake one. Rate depends on your score, your equity, the purpose, and occupancy. Four questions and I’ll give you something I can stand behind. Fair?”

I’m already working with someone

“Good — you should be. I’m not calling to get you to leave them. One question: have you locked yet?”

Their rate is lower than yours

“Could well be. Send me their Loan Estimate — the actual form, not a screenshot. If it genuinely beats mine, I’ll tell you to sign it.”

I want to wait for rates to drop

“Then a second is even better — you get the money now, and we consolidate when rates come down. A cash-out today locks you into today’s rate on everything.”

I need to think about it

“I understand. What else can I give you to help your thinking?” [ SILENCE — say nothing until they speak ]

I need to talk to my spouse

“Absolutely. What do you think their first question will be? And would it be easier if I got you both on for ten minutes so I can answer it directly?”

How did you get my number?

“Fair question. You filled out a request on [site] on [date] and it came through their network. If you don’t remember doing that, say so and I’ll take you off right now.”

My credit’s bad

“Tell me what ‘bad’ means to you — people say that about a 640 and about a 540, and those are completely different conversations.”

Send me something in writing

“Sending it now. I’ll call you at 3:00 to walk through it — those forms are dense and I don’t want you guessing at page two. Does 3:00 work?”

The closing costs are too high

“They’re real money and I won’t pretend otherwise. Three ways: pay them, roll them in, or I take a slightly higher rate and the lender credits them back. Which fits how long you’re staying?”

I’ve never heard of your company

“Fair. Look me up while we’re talking — NMLS Consumer Access, my ID is [number]. Honestly, you should do that with anyone who asks you for a Social over the phone.”

I don’t want to restart my 30 years

“Good instinct. You’re three years in — so instead of resetting to thirty, let’s look at a twenty-five. You cut the payment without giving back the three years you’ve paid.”

I don’t want my card debt on my house

“That’s a real concern. Two things have to be true: the blended rate has to actually drop, and you have to actually close the cards. If you’d run them back up, I’d rather you didn’t do this at all. Can you close them?”

I’m self-employed, I write everything off

“Classic problem — great business owner, poor on paper. If the returns show enough we go conventional. If they don’t, there’s a bank-statement program that ignores tax returns and qualifies you off deposits. Which fits?”

You sound young

“I am. Which is why I check everything twice and answer my phone at nine at night. Ask me anything and see if I know the answer.”

I’m not interested

[ FIRST TIME — one reframe only ] “Totally fair — you don’t know what this is yet. Ten seconds, then you can decide.” [ SECOND TIME — STOP. See section A. ]

Nothing matches. Try “rate”, “spouse”, “credit”, “think”.

Name? → who you are + NMLS → true reason → easy question
2
Frame · 30 sec

3
Discover · 50%
What’s the money for? What happens if you don’t get it?
4
Diagnose
Tell them one true thing they didn’t know
5
Position
Tie back to their words. Get the reaffirming yes.
6
Present
Payment → benefit → delta → rate → then silence
7
Close
Ask for the next small step. Then stop talking.
8
Lock
Time + task + channel. Never “I’ll call you later.”

G

Discovery

Ask, don’t pitch

Situation

How long have you been in the house? Is this your primary residence? Is anyone else part of this decision? ← prevents the spouse objection

Purpose — the one everybody skips

What got you looking at this? What would a successful outcome look like to you? WHAT IS THE MONEY FOR? Is there a timeline, or are you just exploring?

Pain

What happens if this doesn’t get done? How long has that been a problem? What’s it costing you every month right now?

Income — six questions, two minutes

W-2, 1099, or self-employed? → if self-employed: how long? entity? returns filed? “In a normal month, how much is DEPOSITED into the business account? Gross deposits, not profit.” Any other income — rental, retirement, disability? Is any of it non-taxable? ← gross up ×1.25 Any employment gaps in the last two years?

What this means

Everyone who transacted 2020–22 holds 2.5–4%. Rate-and-term refinance is dead. The live business is cash-out, debt consolidation, second liens, and purchase — and it is sold on monthly cash flow and problem resolution, never on rate.

Before every block

The 90-second self-score

Eight or more, consistently, puts you in the top decile of mortgage cold callers. For calibration: the best of the four analyzed transcripts scored 49 out of 110, from an originator with genuinely good instincts.

Currency. Every rate, limit, and regulatory claim here is stamped as of August 2026. Mortgage facts rot fast. Re-verify before you put a number in front of a borrower, and confirm anything compliance-related with your compliance officer — this is training material, not legal advice.
