## The four things you must know cold

1. **Compute a payment in your head.** `(loan ÷ 1,000) × factor`. At 7%, the factor is
   **6.65**. So $285,000 → `285 × 6.65 = $1,895`.
2. **Four things set a rate:** FICO · LTV · occupancy · purpose. Nothing else comes first.
3. **Sub-4.5% first mortgage + needs cash = second lien**, almost always. Never blow up a
   3% rate to pull equity.
4. **Two mandated waiting periods:** Closing Disclosure 3 business days *before* closing;
   rescission 3 business days *after* (refinance on a primary residence only).

## Currency

Everything is stamped **August 2026**. Loan limits change annually, mortgage insurance
factors and funding fees get revised, guidelines shift. **Re-verify any specific number
with your account executive before putting it in front of a borrower**, and confirm
anything compliance-related with your compliance officer. Items that couldn't be verified
are marked `[in brackets]`.
