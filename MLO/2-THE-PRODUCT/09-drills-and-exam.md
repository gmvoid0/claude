## Deck B — Formulas

| Q | A |
|---|---|
| P&I | (loan ÷ 1,000) × factor |
| PITI | P&I + taxes + insurance + MI + HOA |
| LTV | loan ÷ value |
| CLTV | all liens ÷ value |
| DTI | total monthly debt ÷ gross monthly income |
| Max loan from income | ((income × .45 − debts − T&I) ÷ factor) × 1,000 |

| Blended rate | total annual interest ÷ total balance |
| Break-even | cost ÷ monthly savings |
| Gross-up | non-taxable income × 1.25 |

| Self-employed add-backs | net profit + depreciation + depletion + home office |

## Deck D — What moves the rate

| Q | A |
|---|---|
| The 4 questions that determine a rate | FICO · LTV · occupancy · purpose |
| Bond prices rise → rates? | **Fall** (price and yield are inverse) |
| #1 driver of mortgage rates | **Inflation expectations** |
| Does the Fed set mortgage rates? | **No** — indirect only; a cut can push them up |
| FICO down 20 pts across a tier | Rate ↑, MI ↑, payment ↑, DTI ↑ |
| Cash-out vs rate-and-term | Rate ↑, max LTV drops to 80% |
| Primary → investment | Rate ↑↑, down ↑↑, reserves required |
| ARM vs fixed | ARM rate **lower** |
| 15-yr vs 30-yr | 15-yr rate **lower**, payment **much higher** |
| Price above 100 means | Rebate — lender credit available |
| Price below 100 means | Discount — borrower pays points |
| 1 point equals | 1% of the loan amount |
| 1 basis point equals | 0.01% |

# PART 2 — THE DAILY DRILL

**Ten minutes, every day.** Do it out loud.

### Minute 1–2: The factor drill
Say aloud: *"Seven percent is six sixty-five. Six eight seven five is six fifty-seven.
Six seventy-five is six forty-nine."*

Then compute five payments from memory:
- $250,000 at 7%
- $415,000 at 6.875%
- $180,000 at 6.75%
- $325,000 at 7.25%
- $500,000 at 7%

*(Answers: $1,663 · $2,727 · $1,168 · $2,217 · $3,325)*

### Minute 3–4: Deck A
All twenty. Any hesitation = drill again tomorrow.

### Minute 5–6: One reverse-DTI
Make up an income and debts. Compute max loan. Check it.

### Minute 7–8: One product card
Pick one from Deck C. Recite from memory: FICO, down, MI, occupancy, the catch.

### Minute 9–10: One chain
Pick a change ("appraisal comes in low," "borrower buys a car," "FICO drops 20") and
recite the full domino chain out loud.

## After mastery

This guide covers the **product**. Three things extend it, and none of them are in here:

1. **Your lender panel** — which of your ~50 lenders is best for each profile. Build a
   sheet: lender · AE name · niche · turn time · known overlays. Six months of that is
   worth more than any book.
2. **Your pricing engine** — run 20 practice scenarios through it this week. Watch how
   the price moves as you change FICO, LTV, occupancy, and purpose. You'll *see* the
   LLPA grid come alive.
3. **Live guidelines** — everything here is stamped August 2026. Verify current figures
   with your AEs and the agency sites before quoting anything.

**Then re-take the final every 60 days.** Knowledge decays; the drill keeps it.
