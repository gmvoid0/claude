# The 90-Day Ramp

What to do, in what order, from day one.

---

## The shape of it

| Month | Reality | Income |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Learning, dialing, first applications | **$0** |
| **2** | Applications and locks, still nothing landing | Little |
| **3** | First fundings | **Income arrives** |

**Month two is where people quit** — right before the thing they built starts paying.
Budget for 90 days of nothing and keep dialing through it.

---

# Days 1–7 — Don't get fired, don't get sued

**Goal:** know the rules, know your tools, make your first calls.

### Read, in this order
1. `../4-COMPLIANCE/01-cold-calling-law.md`
2. `../4-COMPLIANCE/02-the-trigger-lead-ban.md`
3. `../4-COMPLIANCE/06-the-never-say-list.md`
4. `../3-GO-DEEPER/industry-1-how-a-mortgage-works.md`
5. `../3-GO-DEEPER/industry-5-how-you-get-paid.md`

### Get answers to these — in writing
1. What's my comp in bps? Tiered?
2. LPC, BPC, or both?
3. Per-file or desk fees? W-2 or 1099?
4. Do I pay for leads? What do they cost?
5. When am I paid after funding?
6. **Which states am I licensed in?** *(and build the matrix)*
7. What's our lead vendor's opt-in language? Are leads DNC-scrubbed?
8. What's our recorded-line disclosure policy?
9. Who's my processor? Who's my compliance contact?
10. Whose CRM is it, and what can I take with me?

### Set up
- [ ] CRM access, and understand its dispositions
- [ ] Dialer access — confirm it scrubs DNC automatically
- [ ] Pricing engine access — **run 20 practice scenarios**
- [ ] Lender panel list; start the AE cheat sheet
- [ ] Check whether your outbound number is spam-flagged
- [ ] Write your opening. Say it 50 times out loud.

### Do
- **Make calls on day two.** Not day ten. They'll be bad; that's the point.
- Target: 30 dials/day this week, rising to 50
- Record and listen to one call daily

**Week 1 metric: did you dial every day?** Nothing else matters yet.

# Days 61–90 — Build the machine

**Goal:** consistent production and the beginnings of a self-sustaining pipeline.

### Read
- `../09-business/` — all
- Revisit `../6-PRACTICE/08-what-the-calls-teach.md` with your own calls in mind

### Build
- [ ] **Database cadence running** — quarterly touches, segmented
  (`../5-SALES/07-follow-up.md`)
- [ ] 2–3 real referral relationships in progress
- [ ] Rate-drop list maintained and growing
- [ ] Past-client / closed-elsewhere list being worked
- [ ] A repeatable weekly rhythm you actually follow

### Shift the mix
Month one is 100% cold. By month three you want:

| Source | Target share |
|---|---|
| Cold / aged leads | 70% |
| Database and referrals | 20% |
| Inbound | 10% |

**By month twelve those should roughly invert.** That's the whole game — cold calling is
how you build a database, and the database is what eventually replaces cold calling.

**Targets by day 90:**

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Applications | 8–12/month |
| **Fundings** | **3–4/month** |
| Call score | 75+ |
| Database | 300+ |
| Referral partners | 2–3 active |

## The income math

At $250k average loan and 150 bps (**verify your actual comp**):

| Month | Fundings | Income |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 | $0 |
| 2 | 1 | $3,750 |
| 3 | 3 | $11,250 |
| 6 | 5 | $18,750 |
| 12 | 8 | $30,000 |

> `[Illustrative — see ../10-reference/53-gaps.md]`

**Work backward from your own goal:**

```
Annual goal ÷ comp per file = files/year
÷ 12                        = fundings/month
÷ pull-through              = applications/month
÷ app rate                  = conversations/month
÷ conversation rate         = contacts/month
÷ contact rate              = dials/month
÷ 21                        = DIALS PER DAY
```

That last number is your entire job description.

---

## The traps

| Trap | What happens | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| **Quitting in month two** | Right before it pays | Know the shape going in |
| **Chasing the exciting deal** | One big file eats all your call time | Files in admin time only |
| **Not logging** | Expensive leads thrown away | 30-minute wrap, daily |
| **Changing the script constantly** | Learn nothing | 100-call rule |
| **Avoiding the phone with "prep"** | Feels productive, isn't | Blocks are sacred |
| **Not building the database** | Year two is as hard as year one | Everyone goes in the CRM |
| **Selling rate** | Loses to whoever's cheapest | Sell cash flow and problem resolution |
| **Not recording yourself** | Repeat the same mistake 1,000 times | One call daily |

## The 90-day scorecard

Score honestly at day 90:

- [ ] Dialed every business day
- [ ] Hit 50+ dials consistently
- [ ] Know my contact, conversation, and app rates from memory
- [ ] Can do the per-thousand math in my head
- [ ] Can explain second liens vs. cash-out cold
- [ ] Have never promised a rate before pricing
- [ ] Have honored every revocation immediately
- [ ] Ask what the money is for on every cash-out call
- [ ] Log every call the same day
- [ ] Database over 300
- [ ] Score my own calls weekly
- [ ] 2–3 referral relationships in progress
- [ ] 3+ fundings in month three

**Ten or more: you're going to make it.**

**Under seven: identify which ones and fix those specifically.** It's almost never talent
— it's almost always that a habit didn't get built.
