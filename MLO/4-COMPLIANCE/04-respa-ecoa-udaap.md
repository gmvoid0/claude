# RESPA, ECOA, UDAAP, and Fair Lending

**As of August 2026.** The four rules that govern how you get business and how you treat
people. Violations here end careers faster than anything else in this folder.

---

## 1. RESPA Section 8 — you cannot pay for referrals

**The rule, in one sentence:** you may not give or accept anything of value in exchange
for the referral of settlement service business.

"Anything of value" is interpreted broadly — cash, gifts, trips, event tickets,
below-market rent, free services, paying someone's marketing costs.

Penalties: up to **$10,000 and one year in prison** per violation, plus treble damages.
This is one of the few consumer-finance rules with criminal teeth.

### The realtor problem

Your biggest referral source is real estate agents. Section 8 governs that relationship
completely, and the line is genuinely subtle.

| ❌ Not allowed | ✅ Allowed |
|---|---|
| Paying an agent per referral | Buying them coffee occasionally (nominal, not tied to referrals) |
| Paying their marketing costs | **Co-marketing** where each pays **fair market value** for their share |
| Free CRM, leads, or admin help | Genuine **co-branded** material with proportional cost split |
| Sponsoring their client events outright | Paying fair market value to exhibit at an event |
| Below-market desk rent in their office | **Fair market** desk rent, documented |
| "Preferred lender" status bought with anything of value | Being preferred because you close on time |

**Marketing Services Agreements (MSAs)** are legal in principle — you pay fair market
value for actual, performed, documented marketing services. In practice they are heavily
scrutinized and have produced enormous enforcement actions. **Never enter one without
compliance review.**

> **The test that keeps you safe:** *Am I paying for something of real, independently
> valuable substance at a fair price — or am I paying for the referrals?* If the payment
> would stop being worth it the moment referrals stopped, it's a kickback.

### Affiliated Business Arrangements

If your company has an ownership interest in a title company, escrow, or similar, you
must disclose it via an **AfBA disclosure** at referral, and you may not require the
borrower to use it.

## 2. ECOA / Regulation B — you cannot discriminate

**Prohibited bases:** race, color, religion, national origin, sex (including sexual
orientation and gender identity), marital status, age (if old enough to contract),
receipt of public assistance income, and good-faith exercise of Consumer Credit
Protection Act rights.

### What this means on the phone

You may ask what you need for underwriting. You may not use protected characteristics in
the decision, and you must not discourage.

| ❌ Never say | Why |
|---|---|
| "Are you planning to have children?" | Sex/familial discrimination |
| "Is that child support going to continue?" *(asked to discourage)* | You may ask about reliance and continuance for **qualifying** — but never to discourage |
| "You probably won't qualify, don't bother applying" | **Discouragement** is a violation even without a formal denial |
| "Are you a US citizen?" | You may ask about **permanent residency / work authorization**, not citizenship as such |
| "This neighborhood is tough to lend in" | **Redlining** |

**Discouragement is the trap.** Telling someone informally "your score's too low, don't
waste your time" — if you do that more often to people in a protected class, that's a
violation, and you'll never know you're doing it. **Take the application. Let
underwriting decide.**

### Adverse action

If you deny, or make a counteroffer the borrower doesn't accept, or approve on terms
materially different from what was applied for — an **Adverse Action Notice** must go out
within **30 days**, with the specific principal reasons.

"They just stopped calling me back" is not how you close a file. Ask your processor
whether adverse action is required. Failing to send them is a common, expensive finding
in exams.

### Appraisal rights

Under ECOA, borrowers must receive copies of all appraisals and written valuations
**promptly upon completion**, and no later than **3 business days before consummation**.

## 3. Fair Housing Act

Overlaps ECOA, adds **familial status** and **disability**, and covers advertising and
housing-related services broadly.

Applies to your **marketing**, not just your decisions. Ad targeting that excludes
protected classes — including by geography used as a proxy — is a violation. This has
been a live enforcement area for social media advertising.

**Steering** is the other risk: guiding borrowers toward or away from neighborhoods, or
toward different loan products, based on a protected characteristic. Offering the FHA
product to one demographic and conventional to another with identical files is textbook
steering.

## 4. UDAAP — the catch-all

**Unfair, Deceptive, or Abusive Acts or Practices.** Covered in
`01-cold-calling-law.md` and applied throughout `06-the-never-say-list.md`; here's the
legal structure.

| Prong | Test |
|---|---|
| **Unfair** | Causes substantial injury, not reasonably avoidable, not outweighed by benefits |
| **Deceptive** | Likely to mislead a reasonable consumer, and material |
| **Abusive** | Materially interferes with understanding, or takes unreasonable advantage of a consumer's lack of understanding, inability to protect themselves, or reasonable reliance on you |

**Note what "deceptive" does not require:** intent, or actual harm. If a reasonable
consumer would likely be misled about something material, that's enough.

**The "abusive" prong is the one aimed squarely at sales tactics.** "Taking unreasonable
advantage of the consumer's reasonable reliance on the covered person to act in their
interests" describes exactly what happens when you position yourself as a trusted
advisor and then steer to what pays you best.

## 5. LO Compensation Rule

Covered in depth in `../3-GO-DEEPER/industry-5-how-you-get-paid.md`. The compliance headlines:

- Your compensation **may not vary based on the loan's terms** — not rate, not product,
  not anything but loan amount.
- You may not be paid by **both** the borrower and the lender on the same transaction.
- You may not **steer** a borrower to a loan that pays you more.
- You may not reduce your own comp to cover a pricing concession, except in narrowly
  defined circumstances.

This rule is why a broker's incentives are structurally cleaner than a commissioned bank
LO's — and it is worth explaining to borrowers, because it's a genuine selling point.

## 6. HMDA

Your company reports application-level data — including demographics, pricing, and
outcomes — to regulators, who analyze it for disparities.

**What this means for you:** your denial patterns and pricing patterns are visible in
aggregate. You do not need discriminatory intent to show up in a HMDA disparity
analysis. Consistency in how you treat every borrower is not just ethics; it's
statistical self-defense.

The government monitoring information section is not optional paperwork. Ask for it as
required, explain it plainly, and record refusals correctly.

## Where this touches the transcripts

| Transcript moment | Rule implicated |
|---|---|
| "You sent us information" (borrower denies it) | **UDAAP** — deceptive |
| "I know for a fact I can beat 7.2" (581 FICO, no pull) | **UDAAP** — deceptive; unsubstantiated material term |
| "PennyMac will try not to comply" | **UDAAP** — deceptive disparagement |
| Continuing after three refusals | **TCPA**, and arguably UDAAP-**abusive** |
| "We take scores as low as 500" | **UDAAP** — materially misleading |

## What you must be able to do

- State the RESPA Section 8 test and apply it to a realtor co-marketing offer
- List the ECOA prohibited bases and explain what discouragement is
- Explain the three UDAAP prongs, and which one targets sales tactics
- Explain why you take the application instead of pre-judging

**Self-check:** A top agent offers you "preferred lender" status on all her listings if
you pay half her monthly Zillow bill. Yes or no?

> *No — unless you're getting independently valuable advertising at fair market value,
> documented, and the arrangement survives compliance review. The tell is that the
> payment is priced against the referrals, not against the advertising. Offer instead to
> co-market where you each pay your proportional share and your name appears
> accordingly.*
