# Unit 2 — The Language

The ~110 terms you must own, organized in clusters so related words are learned together.
Each term: plain definition, then the word used in a real sentence you'd actually hear.

**Method:** one cluster per sitting. Copy the cluster by hand. Say each term and its
definition out loud. Flashcards for this unit are in `10-master-drills.md`.

---

## Cluster 1 — The loan itself

| Term | Plain meaning | In a sentence |
|---|---|---|
| **Principal** | The amount borrowed | "Their principal balance is down to $180k." |
| **Interest** | The rent you pay on borrowed money, as a yearly % | "Interest is front-loaded in the early years." |
| **Note rate** | The actual interest rate on the loan | "Note rate is 6.75." |
| **Term** | How many years to pay it off | "30-year term, no prepayment penalty." |
| **Amortization** | The schedule that takes the balance to zero | "It amortizes over 30 even though the ARM fixes for 5." |
| **Note** | The signed IOU | "The note says 6.875 for 360 months." |
| **Lien** | A legal claim on the property securing a debt | "Title found an old contractor's lien." |
| **First lien / second lien** | Payback order if the house is sold in foreclosure — first gets paid first | "The HELOC sits in second lien position." |
| **Equity** | Home value minus everything owed on it | "They've got $250k in equity." |
| **APR** | Rate + certain fees expressed as one comparison number; always ≥ note rate | "The APR is 7.1 against a 6.875 note rate." |
| **Maturity** | The date the loan is scheduled to hit zero | "Matures in 2056." |
| **Prepayment** | Paying extra principal early | "No penalty for prepayment on conventional." |

## Cluster 2 — The payment

| Term | Plain meaning | In a sentence |
|---|---|---|
| **P&I** | Principal + interest — the loan part of the payment | "P&I is $1,895; the rest is escrow." |
| **PITI** | P&I + Taxes + Insurance — the real monthly payment | "Quote PITI, never just P&I." |
| **PITIA** | PITI + Association dues (HOA) | "DSCR uses PITIA." |
| **Escrow / impound** | Account the servicer uses to pay taxes & insurance for the borrower | "Their escrow went up $200 when insurance repriced." |
| **Escrow analysis** | The servicer's annual recalculation of the escrow portion | "The analysis caught a tax increase." |
| **MI / PMI / MIP** | Mortgage insurance — protects the *lender* on low-down-payment loans. PMI = conventional (cancellable), MIP = FHA (usually permanent) | "At 78% LTV the PMI terminates automatically." |
| **HOA dues** | Homeowners association fees — count in the housing payment | "Condo dues are $410 and they count in DTI." |

## Cluster 3 — The ratios (the big three)

| Term | Formula | What it answers |
|---|---|---|
| **LTV** | loan ÷ value | How much cushion does the lender have? |
| **CLTV** | ALL liens ÷ value | Same question when a second exists |
| **DTI** | all monthly debt ÷ gross monthly income | Can they afford it? |
| **Front-end DTI** | housing payment ÷ income | The housing-only version (less used) |
| **Back-end DTI** | everything ÷ income | **The one that matters** |
| **HCLTV** | ALL liens *including full HELOC limit* ÷ value | The stricter CLTV when a line of credit exists |
| **DSCR** | rent ÷ PITIA | Does the rental pay for itself? (investor loans) |

## Cluster 4 — Price and rate

| Term | Plain meaning | In a sentence |
|---|---|---|
| **Point** | 1% of the loan amount | "One point on $300k is $3,000." |
| **Discount points** | Points paid to permanently lower the rate | "She bought the rate down a quarter with one point." |
| **Par** | Price of exactly 100 — no points paid, no credit received | "6.99 is par today." |
| **Rebate / lender credit** | Price above 100 — money available to cover costs, in exchange for a higher rate | "Took an eighth higher and got $2,400 in credit." |
| **LLPA** | Loan-Level Price Adjustment — risk surcharges set by Fannie/Freddie based on FICO, LTV, occupancy, purpose, etc. | "The cash-out LLPA is what's pushing the price." |
| **Rate sheet** | The lender's daily grid of rate vs. price | "Rate sheet worsened 25 bps at 11am." |
| **Reprice** | The lender changing the sheet mid-day when markets move | "We repriced twice today." |
| **Lock** | Freezing a rate for a set number of days | "Locked 30 days at 6.75." |
| **Lock extension** | Buying more days when closing slips | "The extension cost 12.5 bps." |
| **Float** | Not locking; riding the market | "They floated and it moved against them." |
| **Basis point (bps)** | 0.01% — 100 bps = 1% | "Comp is 150 bps." |
| **Margin** | On ARMs: the fixed add-on above the index; also a lender's profit spread | "Index plus a 2.75 margin." |
| **Buydown** | Prepaid money that lowers the rate — temporarily (2-1) or permanently (points) | "Seller funded a 2-1 buydown." |
| **Par rate** | The rate that prices exactly at 100 | — |
| **Overlay** | A lender's own rule that's STRICTER than the agency guideline | "FHA allows 580 but this lender's overlay is 620." |

## Cluster 5 — The process

| Term | Plain meaning | In a sentence |
|---|---|---|
| **Application (the 1003)** | The standard loan application form | "Complete 1003, no blanks." |
| **The six items** | Name, income, SSN, property address, estimated value, loan amount — collecting all six legally = an application | "You've got five of six — careful." |
| **LE (Loan Estimate)** | Standardized cost disclosure, due within 3 business days of application | "LE went out Tuesday." |
| **Intent to proceed** | Borrower's explicit go-ahead after the LE — required before charging fees | "Got intent; ordering the appraisal." |
| **AUS** | Automated Underwriting System — software that pre-decides the file (DU = Fannie's, LPA = Freddie's) | "DU came back Approve/Eligible." |
| **Findings** | The AUS output: decision + required documents | "Findings want 12 months of statements." |
| **Conditions** | The underwriter's to-do list standing between approval and closing | "Three conditions left, all borrower docs." |
| **Conditional approval** | Approved IF the conditions get cleared | — |
| **CTC (Clear to Close)** | All conditions cleared; docs can be drawn | "We're CTC — scheduling the signing." |
| **CD (Closing Disclosure)** | Final cost document — borrower must have it 3 business days before closing | "CD acknowledged Monday, close Thursday." |
| **Funding** | The money actually moves | "Funded and recorded Friday." |
| **Rescission** | 3-business-day right to cancel AFTER closing — refinances of primary homes only | "Refi funds after the rescission period." |
| **Seasoning** | Required waiting time (for funds in an account, after a credit event, after a previous loan) | "The bankruptcy needs one more year of seasoning." |
| **LOE** | Letter of Explanation — borrower's written explanation of anything unusual | "Write the LOE for the late payment and attach the bank letter." |
| **VOE** | Verification of Employment — often re-verified by phone right before closing | "The verbal VOE is the last landmine." |
| **Payoff** | The exact amount to retire an existing loan on a specific date | "Ordered the payoff from the servicer." |
| **Warrantable** | A condo project that meets agency standards | "Non-warrantable condo — different lender pool." |
| **Pull-through** | % of locked loans that actually fund | — |
| **Turn time** | How long a lender takes at each stage | "Their UW turn time is 48 hours right now." |

## Cluster 6 — Credit

| Term | Plain meaning |
|---|---|
| **FICO** | The credit score, 300–850. Mortgage pulls use older models that usually read LOWER than consumer apps. |
| **Tri-merge** | One report from all three bureaus (Experian, Equifax, TransUnion) |
| **Middle score** | Of the three scores, the middle one governs; joint files use the *lower* borrower's middle |
| **Tradeline** | Any account on the report |
| **Utilization** | Balance ÷ limit on revolving cards — ~30% of the score |
| **Derogatory** | Any negative item: late, collection, charge-off, judgment |
| **Mortgage late** | A 30+ day late on a mortgage — the most damaging derog in mortgage lending |
| **Rapid rescore** | Paid, expedited bureau update after paying a balance down — used to jump a pricing tier in days |
| **Hard vs soft inquiry** | Hard = an application ding; soft = no score impact |

## Cluster 7 — Products (preview — Unit 6 goes deep)

| Term | One-liner |
|---|---|
| **Conventional** | The default. Fannie/Freddie rulebook. |
| **FHA** | Government-insured. Easier credit, permanent-ish MIP. |
| **VA** | For veterans. $0 down, no monthly MI. |
| **USDA** | $0 down in eligible rural-ish areas, income caps. |
| **Jumbo** | Above the conforming limit. Private rules, reserve-heavy. |
| **Non-QM** | Alternative documentation: bank statement, DSCR, asset depletion. Not subprime. |
| **HELOAN** | Fixed-rate lump-sum second mortgage |
| **HELOC** | Revolving credit line second, variable rate |
| **ARM** | Fixed for N years, then adjusts (5/6, 7/6, 10/6) |
| **IRRRL / Streamline** | VA's / FHA's fast low-doc refinance — rate reduction only, no cash out |

## Cluster 8 — People and paper

| Term | One-liner |
|---|---|
| **GSE** | Fannie + Freddie collectively |
| **MBS** | The mortgage bond — where rates come from |
| **Servicer** | Collects payments post-closing; often not the original lender |
| **MSR** | The servicing right itself — bought and sold as an asset |
| **AE** | Your rep at each wholesale lender |
| **AMC** | Appraisal Management Company — the middleman that assigns appraisers (keeps them independent of you) |
| **Title insurance** | Protects against ownership defects nobody found |
| **Gift letter** | Signed statement that gifted funds are not a loan |
| **COE** | VA Certificate of Eligibility |
| **NMLS** | The national licensing system — your license number lives here |
