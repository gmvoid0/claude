# How to Study This Guide

Read this page first. It tells you the order, the method, and what to memorize versus
what to merely understand. Ten minutes here saves you weeks.

**What this guide is:** everything about how mortgages actually work — products, numbers,
pricing, and the life of a loan file. Zero sales content. This is the *product knowledge*
track. The `curriculum/` folder holds the longer prose versions; this guide is built for
**memorization and mastery**.

---

## The two kinds of knowledge

Everything in this guide falls into one of two buckets. Study them differently.

| Bucket | Examples | How to study |
|---|---|---|
| **MEMORIZE** (must be instant) | The per-thousand payment table, FICO tiers, product minimums, LTV thresholds, the six application items | Flashcards, daily reps, out loud |
| **UNDERSTAND** (must be explainable) | Why rates track bonds, why MI exists, why cash-out prices worse, how a rate sheet works | Read, then explain it out loud to an empty room |

The test for MEMORIZE items: someone asks, and the answer is out of your mouth in under
two seconds. The test for UNDERSTAND items: you can explain it to a 12-year-old without
notes.

## The daily method

**45 minutes, split three ways:**

1. **15 min — review yesterday.** Out loud. Flashcards from Unit 10 for everything
   already covered.
2. **25 min — new material.** Read the day's section. Every time you hit a MEMORIZE
   table, copy it by hand once. (Handwriting is not optional — it's the fastest
   encoding method known.)
3. **5 min — self-test.** Close everything. Write down the three most important things
   from today from memory. Check. Whatever you missed is tomorrow's first review.

## Spaced repetition — the schedule that makes it stick

Review each unit on this schedule after finishing it:

```
Day 1 (finish it) → Day 2 → Day 4 → Day 8 → Day 21 → Day 60
```

Miss a review and the material starts leaking. Six touches and it's yours for years.
Unit 10 has flashcard decks organized for exactly this.

## The out-loud rule

**Everything you memorize, say out loud.** You are learning this to *use on the phone
and in meetings* — voice-recall and eye-recall are different skills, and you need the
first one. Reciting the FICO tiers in your head is half the rep.

---

## What mastery looks like

You are done with this guide when you can do all of the following cold, no notes:

- [ ] Compute a monthly payment for any loan amount at today's rates, in your head, in
      five seconds
- [ ] Recite every product's minimum FICO, minimum down, and MI treatment
- [ ] Explain what happens to a borrower's rate when their FICO drops 20 points, and why
- [ ] Walk through a rate sheet and build a quote from scratch, including LLPAs and comp
- [ ] Name what every party does to a loan file from application to funding, in order
- [ ] Take any borrower scenario and say: product, obstacles, structure, timeline
- [ ] Pass the 50-question final in Unit 10 at 90%+

That's the bar. Everything in the next ten files is aimed at it.
