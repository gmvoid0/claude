# MLO Product Mastery — Study Guide

A start-to-finish, 0-to-master program on **how mortgages actually work**. Products,
pricing, numbers, qualification, and the full life of a loan file.

**No sales content.** This is the product-knowledge track: what you're selling and how it
works, not how to sell it.

Built for a beginner who intends to **memorize** this, not just read it.

## The four things you must be able to do

If you learn nothing else:

1. **Compute a payment in your head.** `(loan ÷ 1,000) × factor` — at 7%, the factor is
   **6.65**.
2. **Know the four things that set a rate:** FICO, LTV, occupancy, purpose. Nothing else
   comes first.
3. **Know when a second lien beats a cash-out.** Sub-4.5% first mortgage + needs cash =
   second lien, almost always.
4. **Know the two mandated waiting periods:** CD three business days before closing;
   rescission three business days after (refinance on a primary residence).

## Currency

All figures stamped **August 2026**. Mortgage facts rot fast — loan limits change
annually, MI factors and funding fees get revised, guidelines shift. **Re-verify any
specific number with your AE or the agency site before you put it in front of a
borrower.** Items that couldn't be verified are marked `[bracketed]`.
