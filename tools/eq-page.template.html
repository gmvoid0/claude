Map Easy Qualifier

I can't see Easy Qualifier — it sits behind your broker login — so I have no way to learn what its fields are called. This script reads the names of the boxes on screen and hands them back to me. Then S.A.M can drop a finished application straight into EQ.

~5 minutes no code knowledge one paste back to me
eq-probe.js

Nothing to install. This goes into the browser console — step 3.

The steps

  1. Open Easy Qualifier

    Log into UWM as normal and start a scenario. Made-up numbers are fine — a $400,000 house with a $270,000 payoff, VA, cash-out. It never has to be real and you never have to submit it.

  2. Open the console

    Press F12. On a Mac, hold Cmd + Option + I. A panel opens on the right or along the bottom. Click the tab marked Console.

  3. Paste the script in

    Press Copy script at the top of this page. Click the empty space next to the > symbol in the console, paste, and press Enter.

    ! If it says "Allow pasting" — or warns you not to paste code you don't understand — that's Chrome protecting you, and it stops most people on their first try. Type the words allow pasting, press Enter, then paste the script again. You only ever do this once.

  4. Look for the black box

    It appears in the bottom-right corner of the page and looks like this. That means it's working — leave it alone.

    S.A.M — mapping Easy Qualifier
    Reads field names only. Nothing you typed, nothing sent anywhere.
    1 screen, 24 fields
    Copy all & finish

  5. Click through EQ like you normally would

    Fill in your fake scenario and move through every screen. The counter in the black box climbs on its own. Three things worth doing while you're in there:

    • Open every dropdown once — loan type, loan purpose, occupancy, property type, county. Just clicking one open is enough; you don't have to pick anything. These lists are the single most valuable thing you can get me, and some of them don't exist in the page until they're opened.
    • Go all the way to the results screen if you can, so I can see where the rate and payment come out.
    • If a screen doesn't get counted, click Capture this screen in the black box.

  6. Press "Copy all & finish"

    It'll say something like Copied 61KB.

  7. Paste it back to me

    Straight into our chat. It'll be a wall of text — that's exactly what it should look like. If it's too big to paste, press Save as a file instead and send me the .json.

  8. Close it down

    Click close at the bottom of the black box, then press F12 again to shut the console. Nothing is left behind.

What it reads, and what it doesn't

It reads

  • +The names of the fields on screen
  • +What's inside every dropdown — loan types, occupancy, property type. This is the map.
  • +Whether a box is required, greyed out, or formats what you type
  • +Whether a box is empty or has something in it — and nothing more than that

It never reads

  • −Anything you typed. Not a borrower name, not an income figure, not a rate.
  • −Passwords. Those boxes are skipped outright.
  • −Cookies, tokens, or anything else that stands in for your login
  • −Rates, fees or results. No pricing is captured or kept.

It also makes no network calls at all — there isn't a single one in the file. What it collects sits in that one browser tab until you press Copy, and dies when you close the tab. That isn't a promise: the test suite plants a fake session cookie and access token, drives the whole flow, and fails if either reaches the output or if any request leaves the page. It never changes a field, clicks anything, or submits anything either.

If something goes wrong

Nothing happened when I pressed Enter.
Look for red text in the console and send me a photo of it.
The black box never appeared.
You're probably inside a frame. Just above the console input is a dropdown that usually reads top — click it, pick the entry that looks like the Easy Qualifier page, and paste the script again.
It says 1 screen and never goes up.
Click Capture this screen by hand on each screen instead. Same result, just manual.
The fields look wrong to me.
Send it anyway. I can tell from the output whether it read the page properly, and a bad map is far more useful than no map for working out what to fix.

What happens next. I turn what you send into a mapping file, and S.A.M gets a To UWM button: it takes the application you already filled in on the call and drops it into Easy Qualifier's fields in one go. You check it and press Quote yourself.

It stops there, exactly as your spec says. No auto-submit, no handling your credentials, no background requests to UWM. If UWM changes the page and the map stops matching, the button tells you so and falls back to copying the scenario to your clipboard rather than filling in the wrong boxes.
