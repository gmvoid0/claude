# MLO Sales Mastery System

A complete, zero-to-master training system for a new mortgage loan originator —
industry fundamentals, loan products, underwriting, compliance, sales, and above all
**cold calling**.

Built for a **wholesale broker** LO, multi-state licensed, working **purchased internet
and aged leads**, in the **August 2026** market (~6.8% 30-year fixed, trigger leads
banned, cash-out and second liens carrying the business).

## Live coaching

This repo ships Claude Code skills. In any session opened in this directory:

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/mlo-coach` | General coaching — ask it anything, it routes into the curriculum |
| `/cold-call-drill` | Live roleplay. It plays a borrower; you make the call. Then it debriefs you. |
| `/call-score` | Paste a real transcript, get scored 0–100 with specific rewrites |
| `/deal-desk` | Give it borrower facts, get the deal structured — products, math, red flags |
| `/objection` | Instant rebuttal lookup |

`CLAUDE.md` loads the coaching doctrine automatically, so a fresh or forked session
picks up where this one left off without being told.

## A warning about the source material

This system was built partly from four real cold-call transcripts. Those calls were
**trigger-lead calls**, recorded before the Homebuyers Privacy Protection Act took
effect on **March 5, 2026**.

The sales technique in them is frequently excellent. The lead premise — "I'm following
up on an inquiry you submitted" to someone who submitted nothing — is not usable today,
and several specific lines carry real regulatory exposure.

`curriculum/07-teardowns/` separates the technique worth stealing from the premise
that's now unlawful. **Do not lift a script out of those transcripts without reading the
teardown first.**

---

## Currency of the facts

Every rate, limit, and regulatory claim in this system is stamped **as of August 2026**.
Mortgage facts rot fast. Before relying on a specific number in front of a borrower,
re-verify it — and confirm anything compliance-related with your company's compliance
officer. Where a claim could not be verified it is marked
`[UNVERIFIED — confirm with your compliance officer]`.
